Universidad de Manila · 2026

UDM Facility Evaluation & Intervention System

A web-based decision-support tool for Universidad de Manila’s Quality Assurance and Accreditation Division (QUAD). Students give consent, rate 16 service areas on a five-star scale, and leave comments in English, Filipino, or Taglish. Complement Naïve Bayes models classify comment sentiment and service aspects, while rules identify constructive suggestions. QUAD staff use dashboards, alerts, reports, and intervention tracking to review concerns and document follow-up actions.
